Issue 185

This is the End

For now, at least.

Today marks 1,471 days since PT Crab launched, in 2020. July 1, to be exact. That’s 4 years, 10 days. In that time, I’ve sent 184 editions that broke down 536 research articles. Along the way, I’ve saved 1,712 articles and read 1,283. I’ve sent issues to 2,406 different people, racking up more than 250,000 reads in total. It’s been amazing. And now it’s over.

Thank you for reading PT Crab. Thank you for sharing it with friend and colleagues. Thank you for emailing me with your thoughts and ideas. You have all been amazing. I love every last one of you and I’ve loved doing this for you for the last four years. You’ve all been wonderfully supportive and I couldn’t ask for a better audience.

Even with all that wonder behind me, it’s time for something new. I don’t yet know what that is. I need a new writing adventure and the Crab needs to end so I can focus on what’s next. It would do you a disservice to string you all along. I’ve loved doing this, but it’s time for a new path.

Thank you all, again, from the bottom of my heart. You’re wonderful.

Now, the details

If you’re a supporter, I’ll be stopping all monthly renewals and refunding pro-rated annual ones. This may take me a few days and up to a couple of weeks for refunds to be reflected, so be patient but also feel free to nudge me if you don’t see something happen. I’ll get it fixed.

If you’re anyone, I’m going to keep the PT Crab list around and let you know when my new project is going, if it’s relevant to you. Always feel free to email me about just about anything. I’m still here, just not getting in touch every week. The entire archive will remain available at PTCrab.org and open to all (soon, I’m working on fixing the permissions), so you can still refer to it if you want to check in on information. The search tool works very well and it could continue to supplement your patient care, as could the collection of clinical practice guidelines I have.

If you have any other questions, feel free to get in touch at Luke@PTCrab.org, I’ll be there for the foreseeable future. And if you’re in Richmond at some point and want to have a coffee, feel free to shoot me an email and we’ll chat.

The Future

If you know of anyone looking for a writer of my talents, do let them know I’m available. I specialize in the translation of complex information (you’ve seen it, you know it), so if someone needs that done, I’m your guy. I can do it for specialists or a general audience. I’m not job-hunting, per se, but I’m open to opportunity, so feel free to send opportunity my way. I write about just about everything in science and am open to all those topics.

If you want to follow some of what I write outside of PT, check me out on Medium. Examples include:
- Life without your cerebellum
- The time the British tried to turn antelope into farm animals
- The Rubber Duckie map
I hope to create more there in the future, so feel free to follow.

I thank y’all again for helping me build this and I hope you benefited from it over the years. Enjoy your summer and keep being great 💜
