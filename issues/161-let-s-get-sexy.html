Issue 161

Let’s get sexy

In this very sexy interlude between Christmas and New Year’s, we’re going to talk about sex and PT. Most of you probably know that this is a pet issue of mine, it’s really important to me. And I think it should be important to you too. So I’ll try to go some way toward convincing you to care about it too.

We’ll be going through quite a few articles today, some old, some new, that look into sexual activity after injury and/or surgery, a PT’s role in treating survivors of sexual assault (which is more than 20% of the total population), sexuality in people with disabilities, and more.

Also, since this is issue 161, I think it’s time I mention darts. That’s because 161 is a classic, very challenging finish. Most darts is 501, double-out, meaning you have to hit a double to win. For this finish, it’s a triple 60, triple 17, and bullseye. And I mention this because the World Championship of Darts is on right now. Not anywhere that you can watch it really, it’s hard to find. But I got to go to it last week in London and really enjoyed it. They run until January 3rd and I’m a huge darts nerd. Highly recommend learning to play, I find it very meditative.

With that, let’s dive in!
